Hybernate is installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

1. Check the operator and the doorman are running:

   kubectl get pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

2. See which workloads are idle and what they cost, with the kubectl plugin:

   kubectl hybernate scan

3. Opt a workload in, measuring it first so nothing is paused yet:

   kubectl annotate deployment my-app -n my-namespace hybernate.io/dry-run=true
   kubectl label deployment my-app -n my-namespace hybernate.io/managed=true

   Label a namespace instead to opt in everything in it. When you're ready,
   let Hybernate pause it while idle:

   kubectl hybernate enable my-app -n my-namespace

4. Check what it's doing:

   kubectl get managedworkloads -A
